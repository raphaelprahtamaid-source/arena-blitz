Arena Blitz

A small arena shooter for phones and desktops. Everyone with this page open can drop into the same match.

Game type

Everyone for themselves. The last one standing wins the round.

Checking for online play…

Matches are 5 rounds of 1 minute. No respawns during a round: the last player (or team) standing wins it, and most rounds wins the match.
Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims, hold FIRE, tap HEAL for +50 health. Landscape works best.
Desktop: click to lock the mouse, WASD move, Space jump, click to shoot, R reload, E heal.
Friends join by opening this page and typing the same match code.
